About

Jo Nathan is the music project of Johnathan Larroquette — guitarist and producer out of Louisiana, building a catalog that jumps from festival dubstep and trance to heavy guitar covers and raw acoustic songs.

Six singles deep and counting, with live TikTok performances, a growing cast of crowned characters, and new music always cooking. Mask off — this is the real face behind it.
